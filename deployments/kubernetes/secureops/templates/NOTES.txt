The API is reachable at https://{{ .Values.ingress.apiHost }} — which means it is
exposed to whatever can route there. Give CI a `service` token scoped to its own
projects (ADR 023, ADR 033): it is the most widely distributed credential you
have, and it must not be able to switch off the gate that judges it.
{{- end }}

{{- if not .Values.networkPolicy.enabled }}

WARNING: networkPolicy.enabled is false. Every pod can reach every other pod and
the whole network. The worker executes scanner binaries against untrusted target
content; this is the control that keeps it out of your cluster's network.
{{- end }}

A NetworkPolicy is only enforced if your CNI implements it. Confirm rather than
assume — on a cluster that ignores them these objects apply cleanly and do
nothing.
